# Cultural Guidelines for NaijaSingles
## Respectful Representation of African Diaspora Community

---

## 🌍 **Core Principles**

### **1. Authentic Representation**
- Represent African cultures accurately and respectfully
- Avoid stereotypes and generalizations
- Celebrate diversity within African diaspora
- Include voices from various African countries and ethnicities

### **2. Cultural Sensitivity**
- Understand the complexity of diaspora identity
- Respect traditional values while embracing modern perspectives
- Acknowledge the immigrant experience and challenges
- Support cultural preservation and evolution

### **3. Inclusive Community**
- Welcome all African ethnicities and nationalities
- Support various immigration statuses and backgrounds
- Include different generations (first, second, third generation)
- Embrace diverse religious and spiritual beliefs

### **Terminology Guidelines**
- **Use**: "African diaspora," "African-American," "African immigrant"
- **Avoid**: "African-African," outdated terms, generalizations
- **Respect**: Self-identification preferences
- **Include**: Country-specific identities (Nigerian-American, Ghanaian-American)

### **Cultural Context in Messaging**
- **Greetings**: Support cultural greeting customs
- **Names**: Proper handling of African names and pronunciations
- **Titles**: Respect for elders and professional titles

## 🤝 **Community Advisory Board**

### **Recommended Board Composition**
- **Geographic diversity**: Representatives from major US diaspora cities
- **Ethnic diversity**: Various African countries and ethnicities represented
- **Generational diversity**: First, second, and third-generation immigrants
- **Professional diversity**: Various industries and career levels
- **Gender balance**: Equal representation across genders
- **Age range**: Different age groups within target demographic

### **Advisory Board Responsibilities**
- Review cultural representation in app features
- Provide feedback on community guidelines
- Advise on cultural event integration
- Help resolve cultural sensitivity issues
- Guide marketing and outreach strategies
- Support community building initiatives

This document serves as a living guide that should be updated based on community feedback and evolving cultural understanding.
